    The logic

    Thinnest to thickest, always.

    Layering in the wrong order is the most common reason a good routine does nothing.

    01 · Cleanse

    Water-based first, then oil, then balm. Cleansing is the only step where order genuinely matters.

    02 · Treat

    Lightest serums first, thinnest to densest. Give each one 30 seconds to settle before the next.

    03 · Hydrate

    Anything meant to hold water in the skin: hyaluronic serum, then a ceramide cream.

    04 · Protect

    SPF last, every morning, always. It is the only step that prevents rather than repairs.
