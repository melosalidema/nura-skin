How we test

The standard behind every claim we publish — and a plain statement of what is sample data in this concept build.

Disclosure

NŪRA Skin is a fictional brand built as a front-end commerce concept. The figures, studies, reviews and certifications shown on this site are sample data, not real clinical results. Nothing here is a medical claim. Any real product must substantiate its claims with its own testing before they are published.

Stage one

Formulation testing

Every active is trialled at three dose levels; we keep the level that performs under the least generous conditions.

Dose selection

Each active is tested at low, median and trial-range concentrations. The dose that ships is the lowest one that performs, not the one that looks best on a label.

pH & compatibility

Formulas are held within the pH range the actives require, and checked against each other so a routine can be layered without destabilising anything.

Stability & challenge

Twelve weeks at 40°C and 25°C, three freeze-thaw cycles, and a microbial challenge test. A formula that drifts or separates does not launch.

Ophthalmology

Eye-area tolerance is checked separately for the cleanser and SPF, both of which are marketed for use around the eyes.

Stage two

The wear study

200 participants, twelve weeks, self-reported alongside instrument-measured hydration and barrier metrics.

01

Who

200 volunteers across skin types and tones, screened for known sensitivity to any ingredient. Participants with a dermatological condition under treatment are excluded.

02

How long

Twelve weeks, with measurements at baseline, week four, week eight and week twelve. A shorter study cannot show barrier change honestly.

03

What we measure

An instrument-measured hydration and barrier reading, an expert-graded visual assessment, and a participant diary. We report all three, not just the flattering one.

04

What we disclose

Where results were modest, mixed, or dependent on skin type, we say so on the product page. An honest mixed result is more useful than a clean invented one.

What we publish

Percentages, not promises.

The concentration of every lead active is printed on the label and repeated on the product page. This is the one claim a customer can actually act on, so it is the one we lead with.

  • Every key active listed with its exact percentage
  • Every figure linked to an evidence note
  • Sample data clearly marked wherever it appears
